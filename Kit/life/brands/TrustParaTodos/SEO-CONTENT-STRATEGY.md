## 3. Content Rules

1. **800-1500 words per article** — enough to rank, short enough to ship at 80%
2. **Spanish-first** — every article in Spanish, not translated-from-English
3. **One primary keyword + 2-3 secondary keywords** per article
4. **FAQ section at bottom** of every article — for voice search + snippet capture
5. **Internal links:** Every article → `/evaluacion` (CTA) + `/que-es` (hub)
6. **Ship at 80%** — perfect is the enemy of indexed
7. **Target cadence:** 2-3 articles/week minimum
